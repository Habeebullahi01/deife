# Images

Drop your photos here for the gallery.

1. Put them in `images/gallery/` (e.g. `images/gallery/01.jpg`)
2. Update `js/content.js` in the `gallery.items` array with the `src` and a short caption

The gallery will automatically show your photo once you add a `src`. Until then, it shows a little "add a photo" placeholder — exactly as it is now. Keep file names simple (letters, numbers, hyphens).

Suggested sizes: 1200 × 1500 px (4:5 portrait) looks beautiful on this polaroid grid.
